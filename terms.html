Terms of use

Personal is a configuration for its owner's private desktop use, rather than a hosted file service for the public.

Use and responsibility

The owner is responsible for the files and Google account accessed by the desktop client. Changes made through the mounted drive can change or delete cloud files. Uploads may continue after a file is closed because writes are cached locally.

Availability

The setup depends on Windows, local storage, network connectivity, Google Drive, rclone, and WinFsp. Continuous access, specific transfer speeds, and uninterrupted authorization are not guaranteed. The owner should maintain suitable independent backups.

Third-party services and software

Google services, GitHub Pages, rclone, and WinFsp remain subject to their respective terms and licenses. Personal is independent of Google, GitHub, and the upstream software projects.
